Demo: Amazon S3 multipart upload

Multipart upload divides a large object into independently uploaded parts.

Key observations:
- Failed parts can be retried without restarting the entire upload.
- Parts can be uploaded concurrently.
- All uploaded parts must be completed or explicitly aborted.
- Incomplete parts can create storage charges.
- A lifecycle rule can abort incomplete multipart uploads after a chosen number of days.

This is a small conceptual sample and does not itself force multipart behavior.
