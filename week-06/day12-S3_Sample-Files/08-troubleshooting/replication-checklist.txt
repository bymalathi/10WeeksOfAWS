S3 Replication Troubleshooting Checklist

1. Confirm versioning is enabled on the source and destination buckets.
2. Confirm the replication rule is enabled.
3. Confirm the object key matches the configured prefix or tag filter.
4. Confirm the object was uploaded after the rule became active.
5. Check the source object's replication status.
6. Confirm the replication IAM role can read the source and write to the destination.
7. For SSE-KMS objects, verify source and destination KMS key permissions.
8. Confirm the destination bucket and Region are correct.
9. Remember that replication is asynchronous and can remain Pending temporarily.
10. Use S3 Batch Replication for eligible pre-existing objects.
